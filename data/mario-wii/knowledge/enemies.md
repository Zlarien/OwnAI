# Enemies

A Goomba is a weak, mushroom-shaped enemy that walks slowly in a straight line. Mario defeats a Goomba by jumping on it or by using a power-up such as a fireball.

A Koopa Troopa is a turtle-like enemy. When Mario jumps on a Koopa Troopa, it retreats into its shell. The shell can then be kicked to slide along the ground and knock out other enemies.

A Buzzy Beetle is a beetle enemy with a hard shell. Buzzy Beetles are immune to fireballs, so Mario must stomp them or use another method to defeat them.

A Piranha Plant is a carnivorous plant that emerges from green warp pipes, snapping its jaws. It can be defeated with fireballs or by hitting it while invincible.

A Lakitu is an enemy that rides on a cloud and throws Spiny eggs down at Mario. The Spiny eggs hatch into Spinies, which cannot be safely stomped because of their spikes.

A Dry Bones is a skeletal Koopa. When Mario stomps a Dry Bones, it collapses into a pile of bones but reassembles itself after a few seconds. Dry Bones cannot be defeated by stomping alone.

A Hammer Bro is a tougher Koopa that jumps around and throws a steady stream of hammers at Mario. Fireballs or a Star are effective against Hammer Bros.
