# Gameplay

New Super Mario Bros. Wii is a side-scrolling platform game released for the Nintendo Wii in 2009. Players guide Mario through a series of levels grouped into eight main worlds plus a secret ninth world.

A major feature of the game is simultaneous multiplayer. Up to four players can play at the same time as Mario, Luigi, and two colored Toads. Players can help each other or get in each other's way by picking each other up and throwing one another.

The game is controlled by holding the Wii Remote sideways. The player runs by holding a button and jumps with another. Shaking the Wii Remote performs a spin, activates the Propeller form, or picks up other players.

Each level contains three large Star Coins hidden in tricky locations. Collecting Star Coins unlocks extra levels and the paths to secret exits. Finding every Star Coin is required for full completion of the game.

Levels end at a flagpole. Reaching the top of the flagpole awards extra points and, depending on timing, extra lives. Some levels also contain a hidden secret exit that opens an alternate path on the world map.

When all players lose their lives, the game offers a Super Guide. If the player fails a level many times, a green block appears that lets Luigi play through the level automatically to demonstrate how to complete it.
