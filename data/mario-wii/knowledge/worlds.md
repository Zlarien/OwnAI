# Worlds and Bosses

The game has eight numbered worlds, each with its own theme, plus a secret World 9 unlocked by collecting Star Coins.

World 1 is a grassy plains area that introduces the basic mechanics. World 2 is a hot desert with quicksand and pyramids. World 3 is a frozen, icy world with slippery surfaces. World 4 is a water world with beaches and underwater levels.

World 5 is a jungle full of poisonous swamps and vines. World 6 is a rocky, mountainous world. World 7 is a sky world set high in the clouds. World 8 is Bowser's lava-filled domain, the final world before the last battle.

At the end of the first castle in most worlds, Mario fights one of the seven Koopalings as a boss. The Koopalings are Larry, Morton, Wendy, Iggy, Roy, Lemmy, and Ludwig. Each Koopaling has a slightly different attack pattern.

The Koopalings first appear in an airship battle at the end of each world, then again in the world's castle. Kamek, a magic-using Magikoopa, often enchants the arena to make the second fight harder.

The final boss is Bowser. In the last castle Bowser is first fought at a normal size. After being defeated, Kamek uses magic to make Bowser enormous, and Mario must flee and press a switch to finish him.
