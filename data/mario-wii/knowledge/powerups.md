# Power-ups

The Super Mushroom turns Small Mario into Super Mario, making him larger and letting him take one extra hit before losing a life.

The Fire Flower gives Mario the Fire form, allowing him to throw fireballs that defeat most enemies from a distance.

The Ice Flower gives Mario the Ice form, letting him throw iceballs that freeze enemies into blocks of ice. Frozen enemies can be picked up and thrown.

The Propeller Mushroom is exclusive to New Super Mario Bros. Wii. It gives Mario the Propeller form. By shaking the Wii Remote, Mario spins upward into the air, then slowly descends. It is very useful for reaching high platforms and coins.

The Penguin Suit turns Mario into Penguin Mario. In this form Mario slides on his belly across ice and water, swims much better, and can throw iceballs like the Ice Flower.

The Super Star makes Mario invincible for a short time. While invincible, Mario cannot be hurt by enemies or most hazards, and touching enemies defeats them. The background music speeds up while the Star is active.

The Mini Mushroom shrinks Mario down to a tiny size. Mini Mario can run across water for a short time and fit through very small gaps, but is defeated by a single hit.
